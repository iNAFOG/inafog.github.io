DIAGNOSE: fairness-aware multi-teacher distillation for medical imagingSep 2025 – Present

WiML @ NeurIPS 2026Under review: IJMI

  • Problem: debiasing methods for medical image classifiers usually trade accuracy for fairness and are evaluated on a single dataset and a single sensitive attribute.
  • Approach: proposed and built DIAGNOSE, a multi-teacher knowledge distillation framework where one student learns both predictive performance and demographic impartiality.
  • Results: state-of-the-art fairness on PAPILA glaucoma detection (9.7 DPD, 27.0 EOD; lower is better) and 85.3 worst-group AUROC with a 2.2-point skin-type gap on Fitzpatrick17k, holding up in low-sample, class-imbalanced settings.

LLM Inference Engine

Hand-written decode loop, KV cache and samplergithub.com/iNAFOG/InferenceEngine
  • Autoregressive decode loop with a KV cache and its own temperature, top-k and top-p sampler. Output matches Hugging Face generate() token-for-token at equal throughput (32.6 vs 32.8 tokens/s).
  • On Qwen2.5-0.5B with a GTX 1650: KV caching gives a 2.47× decode speedup at 256 tokens, and batching raises throughput 5.67× at batch size 8 (31 to 176 tokens/s).
  • Single-request decode is bound by memory bandwidth and kernel-launch overhead, not compute: KV caching cuts tokens processed 131× but yields 2.47×, while batching scales almost linearly.
  • Analytical memory model validated against measurement: 1,885 MiB predicted vs 1,894 MiB measured for FP32 weights (FP16: 942 vs 958 MiB).
  • Batched decoding for variable-length prompts (left padding, attention masks, position ids) and UTF-8-safe NDJSON token streaming, verified by 28 pytest tests.

DIAGNOSE: Fairness-Aware Neural Distillation

Multi-teacher distillation for fair medical imaginggithub.com/iNAFOG/DIAGNOSE
  • Designed and implemented the full multi-teacher distillation framework: one student learns predictive performance and demographic impartiality from several teachers.
  • Custom distillation loss with group-aware standardisation that targets worst-group performance. The student keeps the inference cost of a single standard model.
  • Shared training and evaluation pipeline over 5+ clinical datasets (PAPILA, CheXpert, Fitzpatrick17k, OASIS), reporting DPD, EOD and worst-group AUROC by age, sex and skin type.
